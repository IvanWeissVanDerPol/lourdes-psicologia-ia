## 2. Core Relational Needs

### A. The Need for "Containment"

- **Definition:** The need for a partner to hold space for her anxiety without crumbling or fleeing.
- **Evidence:** She seeks out his presence specifically when she is "spiraling" (high anxiety).
- **Failure Point:** Victor withdraws (Deactivation) exactly when she needs containment (Hyperactivation), causing the spiral to worsen.

### B. The Need for "Object Constancy"

- **Definition:** Knowing the relationship exists even when not interacting.
- **Deficit:** Patient lacks object constancy; "Out of sight, out of mind" becomes "Out of sight, out of love."
- **Quote:** "Me siento sola... nadie me entiende." (File: `00000694`)

### C. The Need for "Predictability"

- **Definition:** Consistency in response time and tone.
- **Observation:** She thrives on routine and crashes on ambiguity.
- **Recommendation:** Establish "Rituals of Connection" (e.g., a guaranteed Good Night text, regardless of the day's mood).

### D. The Need for Competence/Utility (The "Ivan" Gap)

- **Definition:** The need for practical help and problem-solving without emotional cost.
- **Observation:** Victor fails here ("No sé", "Veremos"). Ivan succeeds here (Fixing PC, advice).
- **Risk:** Lourdes outsources this need to Ivan, which might lower the friction with Victor but also lowers the interdependence/trust.

## 3. "Translation" Script for Therapy

**Goal:** Teach Patient 001 to ask for the _Need_, not the _Strategy_.

- **Instead of:** "You never listen to me!" (Accusation/Strategy)
- **Try:** "I am feeling really anxious right now and I just need you to listen for 2 minutes to feel better." (Need)

- **Instead of:** "Are you angry?" (Reassurance Seeking)
- **Try:** "I'm telling myself a story that you are mad. Can you just give me a thumbs up if we are good?" (Reality Testing)
