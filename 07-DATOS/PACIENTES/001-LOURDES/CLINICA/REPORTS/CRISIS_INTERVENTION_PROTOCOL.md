# Crisis Intervention Protocol: Patient 001 (Lourdes)

> **Context:** "Noviembre Negro" (The Black November) - A reference to previous simultaneous crises (Pet illness + Exam failure).
> **Objective:** A deployable safety plan for high-distress periods.

---

## 1. The "Red Zone" Indicators

Signs that the patient is entering a crisis state:

1.  **Somatic Collapse:** Check for mentions of "dolor de cabeza" (headache), "no puedo respirar" (can't breathe), or "cansancio extremo" (extreme fatigue).
2.  **Audio Flooding:** Sending >5 mins of audio in <10 mins.
3.  **Catastrophic Language:**
    - "No aguanto más" (I can't take it anymore).
    - "Todo sale mal" (Everything goes wrong).
    - "Me quiero morir/desaparecer" (I want to die/disappear - _Passive Suicidal Ideation Check_).

---

## 2. The Protocol: "D.E.E.P."

### **D - De-escalate (Physiological)**

- **Immediate Action:** Stop interacting with the Partner.
- **Technique:** TIPP (Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Paired muscle relaxation).
- _Specific Recommendation:_ Ice water on the face (to trigger Dive Reflex).

### **E - Externalize (The Problem)**

- **Cognitive Shift:** "I am not the problem. The _situation_ is the problem."
- **Action:** Write down the 3 facts of the situation. (e.g., "My dog is sick. I failed a test. Victor is busy.") Separating them prevents the "Cluster Overwhelm."

### **E - Engage (Support System)**

- **Rule:** Do NOT call Victor as the primary soothing agent during crisis (he is likely the trigger or unavailable).
- **Action:** Call a designated "Safe Friend" or Family Member (Mother is frequently mentioned: `Family References` - 61 hits).

### **P - Plan (Next 1 Hour)**

- **Focus:** Survive the next 60 minutes.
- **Task:** Do one mechanical task (e.g., Wash dishes, Walk the dog).
- _Why:_ Engages the prefrontal cortex and dampens the amygdala hijack.

---

## 3. Digital Boundary Rules (The "Victor" Firewall)

### Rule A: The "One Minute" Rule

- **Trigger:** Urge to explain yourself via audio.
- **Action:** If the audio is > 1 minute, **DELETE IT**.
- **Reason:** Long audios are perceived as "attacks" or "noise" by avoidant partners, triggering _their_ withdrawal and fueling _your_ anxiety.

### Rule B: The "Airplane Mode" Tactic

- **Trigger:** Victor does not reply for > 2 hours.
- **Action:** Put phone on Airplane Mode for 30 minutes.
- **Reason:** Removes the possibility of the "ding" dopamine hit. You regain control of the silence.

### Rule C: The "Third Party Check" (Anti-Triangulation)

- **Trigger:** Temptation to mention a "Special Person" (Ivan) to make Victor jealous.
- **Action:** Stop. Write down: "I am feeling invalid. I want to hurt him back."
- **Reason:** Mentioning third parties only confirms Victor's narrative that _you_ are unstable/unfaithful. It backfires 100% of the time.
