# Cognitive Distortion Analysis: Patient 001 (Lourdes)

> **Clinical Approach:** CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)
> **Goal:** Identify and restructure maladaptive thinking patterns.

---

## 1. Dominant Distortions

### A. Personalization (The "It's Me" Virus)

**Definition:** Taking responsibility for events outside one's control (specifically partner's mood/actions).

- **Evidence:** "Si estás cansado... es mi culpa." (If you are tired... it's my fault).
- **Pattern:** Interprets partner's fatigue or stress as a reaction to _her_.
- **Reframing:** "He is tired because of work/family. It has nothing to do with me."

### B. Emotional Reasoning

**Definition:** Believing that because something _feels_ true, it _is_ true.

- **Evidence:** "Siento que no te importo" -> Treats this feeling as a fact that requires a defense.
- **Quote:** "Me siento más hipersensible... y por eso..."
- **Reframing:** "I feel anxious, but that doesn't mean the relationship is in danger. Anxiety is a feeling, not a fact."

### C. Mind Reading

**Definition:** Assuming knowledge of what the other person is thinking (usually negative).

- **Evidence:** "Hay veces que no respondes... para mí me está diciendo que..." (You don't answer... to me that says that...)
- **Pattern:** Fills the silence with a negative narrative constructed by the insecurity.
- **Reframing:** "I don't know why he isn't answering. I will wait and ask, instead of guessing."

### D. Catastrophizing

**Definition:** Believing the worst-case scenario will happen.

- **Context:** Fear that a minor conflict will lead to total abandonment ("No quiero que me pase de nuevo" - I don't want it to happen to me again).

---

## 2. CBT Worksheet: The "Record Button" Technique

The patient loves voice notes. Use this to her advantage.

**Exercise:** "The Draft Folder"

1.  **Trigger:** Patient wants to send an anxious voice note.
2.  **Action:** Record it, but **send it to herself** (or a private group).
3.  **Review:** Listen to it an hour later.
4.  **Audit:** Identify one "Mind Reading" or "Personalization" in the audio.
5.  **Re-record:** Record a shorter, fact-based version. Send _that_ one.
